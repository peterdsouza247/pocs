# Thrum — development roadmap

- 0.1.0: initial six-puzzle browser slice; procedural placeholder art and authored guidance. DONE.
- 0.2.0: DONE (first interactive pass): adjustable mechanisms, bell sequence, hero swap and tap-to-position, saved mechanism state, visual cues, reset, elegant-solution stars. Independent assist and full real multitouch remain open; carry them into 0.7.0. No general physics engine yet.
- 0.3.0: bespoke detailed pixel art for village, sprites, title, portraits, icon, transitions; accessible illustrated notebook; sound design and adaptive music; calm tutorial; local device save and performance tests. Full build pipelines Android/iOS/desktop.
- 0.4–0.6: Reedwater, Glimmerdeep, Thistledown Terraces, Emberforge, Hollow Heart in waves; varied interactions and no idle second player; region memory scenes, narrative arcs and restoration effects. Preserve the original standalone world.
- 0.7–0.9: family playtesting, independent assist, replay, accessibility, balancing, save migration, localization, hardware testing, offline packaging and release candidate. Science and home experiment safety review before publication; no required sound or reading.
- 1.0: six regions, 50 puzzles, complete story, no punitive failure, full mobile builds and publisher-ready presentation. Release pricing remains open.
